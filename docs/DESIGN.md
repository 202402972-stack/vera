# VÉRA visual identity

Reference inspected in mobile and desktop: https://pixells.co/en. Layout and palette are deliberately drawn from the requested reference; VÉRA copy, vector mark, product screenshots and boutique assets are separate. No Pixells logo or photographer portfolio imagery is shipped.

| Token | Value | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Canvas | #F2F1EF | Hero and quiet background sections |
| Primary | #743F37 | Primary buttons, headings, statement section |
| Gold | #B49473 | Editorial emphasis and decorative accents |
| Ink | #4A3A33 | Core text |
| Muted | #737365 | Supporting copy |
| Highlight | #F0E4AF | CTA/text on the brown statement section |
| White | #FFFFFF | Content, cards and negative space |
| Navigation | rgba(67,48,29,0.5) | Translucent hero navigation |

Hero composition: cropped upper central image, side images surrounding a centered badge/title/copy/CTA on desktop; badge/title/CTA followed by laptop-framed store preview on mobile. The storefront retains its independent brand token contract and original light-gold shopping buttons.

English: Playfair Display headings and Raleway body. Arabic: Amiri headings and Tajawal body. Fonts are locally hosted; licenses are bundled. The reference's private Gotham Narrow font is not redistributed.

The monogram is a three-part geometric V, unchanged between Arabic and English. `public/platform/assets/vera-mark.svg`, `public/platform/vera-profile.svg`, and the 1080px PNG are included. VÉRA is a proposed brand name; no trademark/domain registration is claimed.

`/design-system` displays the live palette/type samples and downloadable assets. Breakpoints cover narrow phones, tablets and desktop. Forms use logical positioning for password controls in RTL. Motion is restricted to subtle entry/hover transitions and disabled for reduced-motion preference.
